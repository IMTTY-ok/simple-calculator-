Calculator is a premium, minimal calculator built to feel like a real piece of hardware — and to stay out of your way while you work.

PRETTY NUMBERS, EVERY TIME
• A large, high-contrast display with tabular figures that never shift or reflow as you type.
• Live result preview: see where a calculation is heading before you press equals.
• Full expressions stay visible, so you always know what you are looking at.

REAL HAPTIC FEEDBACK
Every interaction has its own feel on Android: a light tap for numbers, a firmer one for operators, a confirmation pulse for equals and a distinct buzz for errors. A subtle synthesised click is available too, and both can be switched off.

BUILT FOR REAL CALCULATIONS
• Correct operator precedence — 2 + 3 × 4 is 14, not 20.
• Percentages that behave the way you expect: 200 + 10% is 220, and 200 × 10% is 20.
• Decimals, negative numbers, backspace, clear entry and clear all.
• Repeated equals repeats the last operation, so 2 + 3 = = gives you 8.
• Division by zero is reported clearly instead of showing nonsense.
• Very large and very small numbers are formatted properly, never as floating-point noise — 0.1 + 0.2 is 0.3.

CALM AND CAPABLE
• A refined dark theme and an equally considered light theme.
• The last 50 calculations are stored on your device so you can reuse a result, or delete them at any time.
• Full keyboard support on a laptop or tablet.
• Accessible by design: large touch targets, clear focus states, screen-reader friendly announcements and reduced-motion support.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Calculator asks for no permissions and collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Your history and settings live on your device and stay there — Android cloud backup is switched off on purpose. The app works completely offline, in airplane mode, with no connection at all.

FAST, LIGHT, HONEST
The entire app is under 2 MB, opens instantly, and is verified by an automated test suite: a Python reference engine calculates the same results using exact decimal arithmetic, and every release is checked against it.

No sign-up. No subscription. No nonsense. Just a calculator that does the arithmetic properly.
