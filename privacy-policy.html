Calculator

Privacy Policy

Calculator is a calculator, not a service. It has no account system, no backend and no analytics. Everything you type stays on your device.

Short version

No data is collected, no data is shared, and nothing is sent anywhere. The app performs every calculation on your device and stores your preferences and history only in your device's own local storage. It works with no internet connection at all.

What the app does with your calculations

Every arithmetic operation is performed locally, inside the app, using a token-based expression engine that runs entirely in the app's own JavaScript runtime. Your numbers are never transmitted, logged or uploaded.

What is stored on your device

Two small pieces of information are kept, both in the platform's local storage (localStorage) on your device only:

What Why Where it lives
Settings Theme, haptic and sound preferences, reduced-motion preference Local storage on your device
History The last 50 calculations, so you can reuse a result Local storage on your device

This data never leaves the device. It is not uploaded, not synchronised, and not backed up to a cloud service. On Android, the application disables Android's backup and device-transfer mechanisms, so even the operating system does not copy it to a cloud backup or to another device.

No account, no server, no third-party services

Calculator does not use, and does not require:

  • User accounts, sign-in or any authentication service
  • Remote servers, APIs or backend databases
  • Cloud storage or file-syncing services
  • Analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising SDKs
  • Advertising, tracking or profiling of any kind

Permissions

The application requests no runtime permissions. It does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or any other sensor.

The Android package declares the standard internet permission. It is required by the Android WebView runtime that renders the interface; the app does not use it to make any network request. All of the application's code, fonts and images are bundled inside the installed application.

Offline by design

The application is fully functional with no internet connection, including in airplane mode. After the first launch it does not need a network connection at all: the interface, the fonts and the calculator engine are all stored on the device.

Children

Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it is safe for all ages. It contains no ads, no purchases and no links to external content.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects data handling, this policy will be updated in the same release as the change, and the "Last updated" date below will change.
